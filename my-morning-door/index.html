About

Why I made My Morning Door

Hello, I’m Christin.

On busy days, I often move straight into everything waiting for me before remembering to pause, breathe, or ground myself. I wanted something simple that would already be there when I opened my browser, without becoming another task or habit to manage.

My Morning Door offers a short, optional moment to settle before continuing. This project is my small contribution toward making everyday technology more supportive of different ways of thinking and processing information.
