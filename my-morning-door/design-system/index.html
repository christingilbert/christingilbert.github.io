Design system

How My Morning Door stays calm.

My Morning Door is a multi-sensory Chrome new tab for a calmer start. It combines photography, locally bundled ambient sound, optional voice guidance and short practices without becoming another system to maintain. This page explains the visual, interaction, accessibility and copy choices behind the shipped product.

  • Senses sight, sound and body, all optional
  • Face Atkinson Hyperlegible Next
  • Grounds dark glass over photographs
  • Targets 44 px, 48 px on touch
  • Motion Full, Gentle, Still
  • Data stays on the device

If you are reading this from the portfolio, start with the experience model, principles, components, accessibility and copy rules. The technical reference and maintenance notes are included for future edits and sit outside the main reading path.

How it fits together

Experience model

The tab can be shaped through sight, sound and body. Each part can stand on its own, each starts quiet or off, and nothing is kept beyond the browser.

Sight

A visual place to land

A built-in photograph, or one of your own, fills the screen. The glass panel holds the words, and the living circle gives the page a slow centre. Text sits on measured surfaces, the typeface is chosen for readability, and motion can be full, gentle or still.

Sound

Sound only when you ask for it

Ocean, soft rain, forest and brown noise are bundled with the extension, so they play without streaming. Sound starts only after a press, lowers under voice guidance, and can stay with you for 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or until you stop it. The door icon by the address bar shows what is playing.

Body

Short practices without a record

Breathing (a no-hold default, or Box Breathing with clearly disclosed holds), sensory grounding and seated muscle release are available when they help. Voice guidance is optional, the pace can be manual or automatic, and every step has a visible way out. There are no counts, streaks or history.

The quiet tab shows a greeting, one line and one button. The full pause appears at the start of a window or after a long break, as long as that preference stays on.

Why it looks like this

Five principles

These are the decisions that keep the product calm. For a change that the sections below leave open, come back here first.

1

The threshold asks nothing

A new tab should feel like a place to arrive. There are no counters, streaks or end-of-day prompts. On the quiet tab, the address bar keeps focus so someone can keep typing and move on.

Rule: the resting tab asks for nothing and offers one clear button.

2

One surface at a time

The photograph is the room. The glass panel is where the words live. When two blurred surfaces stack, or when a panel flickers, the room stops feeling settled.

Rule: only one backdrop-filter surface can be active on screen at a time.

3

Equal-weight choices

Leaving gets the same respect as continuing. "Continue quietly" sits beside "Keep the ocean", at the same size and weight. Exit links have real touch targets.

Rule: every step has a visible way out, styled as a real control with a real target.

4

Nothing startles

No autoplay, tap flash, bounce or sudden white. Sound starts after a choice. Text changes with a small transition.

Rule: the default state is off, still or quiet. The person turns things on.

5

Readable over changing photographs

The typeface is chosen for readability, but the surface does the harder work. Text sits on dark glass, a light pill or a vignette. Personal photos get a stronger floor because their contrast cannot be predicted.

Rule: text always sits on a surface. The one exception is the resting greeting on the four curated dark photographs.

What to resist. The product itself has no accent colour, no illustration style, no card grid and no icon set beyond the three practice marks. Restraint is the identity, and adding a hue or a fourth surface would be a redesign. This documentation page uses a small sea-teal accent only for navigation and system notes; it belongs to this page alone.

Foundations

Type

One face for everything: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, the variable Latin file (200 to 800), bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License, loaded with font-display: swap. It was chosen for low-vision legibility: unambiguous letterforms, open counters, a real distinction between I, l and 1. No webfont request ever leaves the device.

The type system stays simple. Headings are tight and calm, labels are small and open, and body text stays plain. The goal is to make the next action easy to read.

Foundations

Brand and icons

The mark is a closed double door under a Moroccan-inspired ogee arch, based on Christin's Illustrator artwork. It is treated as a quiet presence. It appears as a filled mark over photographs, a line version for hero use, and the store icon on door ink.

Rules for the mark

  • The resting mark is drawn on the canvas at 1.72 r of the visual anchor, tinted to --paper, at 0.8 alpha with a slow 15 second breath. It is white over the four dark photographs.
  • The mark stays out of pills and hover states. It is a brand mark and behaves like one. When shown small in the header it uses the drop shadow above, nothing else.
  • The store icon keeps the full treatment: door ink square (radius 112 of 512), warm-white disc, door in ink. Sizes 16, 48, 128, 180, 192, 512 and maskable 192 and 512 ship as PNG.
  • The wordmark is live text: "My Morning" at 560 and 82% opacity, "Door" at 790. It sits fixed bottom-left, 24 px from the edges (14 px on phones), and always stays on screen, because a shared link may open the page for someone meeting the product for the first time.
  • The mark is inlined as a CSS mask. A linked SVG mask is blocked on an opaque origin and silently vanishes, so inlining keeps it visible however the page is opened.

Practice icons

Three marks, drawn on a 48 grid with a 3.8 stroke that keeps its width at every size, round caps and joins. They take the text colour (currentColor in the CSS), so one file serves every surface. They sit in a 58 px disc of white 0.15 with a 1 px inset ring of white 0.24. Each has a small hover movement, described in the Motion System.

The only icons in the product, on their discs

Breathing, Sensory grounding, Seated muscle release. Chair stroke 5.4, body stroke 7.

Two utility glyphs exist for narrow screens only: the speaker (off and on) and the gear, both 24 grid at 1.8 stroke, masked in #112122 on the paper pill. Labels that fit as words stay as words.

Rules for a new photograph

  • Dark overall, with no bright patch where the greeting sits (centre) or where the panel sits (right on desktop, lower half on phones). Every built-in photograph is treated with contrast(0.98) brightness(0.9) and a bottom gradient of 0.16.
  • Give it a crop position. The rocky shoreline is anchored at 0% because the interest is on the left.
  • WebP, sized for a 2560 longest edge at most. The four presets weigh 63 to 264 KB.
  • Personal uploads: JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 15 MB; resized to 2560 max edge, re-encoded to WebP at 0.86, stored in IndexedDB on the device only. Position choices: left 25%, centre 50%, right 75%.
  • Preset tiles in Preferences show the photograph with a bottom gradient of rgba(5,16,17,0.72) and a 14 px 760 label; the pressed tile gets a 3 px white 0.95 border and a 3 px white 0.22 ring.

Choose your pause

What would help right now?

Three short practices are here. None of them are required, and you can leave any of them at any point.

  • Kicker names the screen. It is removed on short phones because the options repeat it.
  • Title is one question or one instruction, at most 12.5ch wide, balanced.
  • Lead is one or two plain sentences in mist. Max 52ch.
  • Options follow after 34 px. Three at most.
  • Exit is an underlined text button with a 48 px target.

Resting threshold

The tab that asks nothing: a greeting, one supporting line, one pill. No panel, because the type sits directly on the dark photograph at 16:1 or better. On personal photographs a radial vignette (rgba(6,15,16,0.68) to transparent at 88%) appears behind the cluster; with reduced transparency the cluster sits on one opaque 28 px panel.

Good afternoon.

A short breathing, grounding, or muscle-relaxation practice is here if you need it.

Rules that hold everywhere

Targets

44 px minimum for anything tappable, 48 px on touch and for header pills, 52 px for the primary. Range sliders are 44 px tall with a 26 px thumb on touch.

Focus

3 px solid outline in --focus, offset 4 px, with a 4 px dark under-ring so it survives bright photographs. Never removed. Chips move the ring to the label with :focus-within.

Names and roles

Ambient and Voice guidance are role="switch" with aria-checked. Preferences has an aria-label. Presets and rhythm options use aria-pressed. The dialog is labelled by its title. One polite live region announces state.

Motion

Full, Gentle, Still in Preferences. Still and prefers-reduced-motion render one static frame and stop the animation loop. An explicit in-app choice wins over the system setting.

Transparency

prefers-reduced-transparency swaps every glass for an opaque panel and every light pill for solid paper.

Nothing autoplays

Sound and voice are off by default. The install video is off until pressed. Guided steps auto-advance only after the person chose "automatic".

Touch

touch-action: manipulation removes the double-tap delay while keeping pinch zoom. Tap highlight is off, overscroll bounce is off, inputs are at least 16 px, so Safari keeps the layout still.

Focus management

Each new screen moves focus to its title (tabindex="-1"). Focus goes only to the live title. The resting tab leaves focus in the address bar, so typing reaches it.

Colour scheme

The tab declares color-scheme: dark, the popup and install page declare light, so native controls and scrollbars match their surface.

Rules

Voice and copy

The words have to be as quiet as the interface. They stay short, plain and non-demanding, so someone with little capacity can understand a screen quickly.

Tone

Warm, unhurried, second person, and phrased as an offer: "is here if you need it", "only if it feels comfortable", "or keep breathing in your own way".

Sentences

One idea per sentence. Everyday words. On-screen copy avoids product jargon such as "session", "mode" or "feature".

Punctuation

No em dashes anywhere: interface, metadata, docs, code comments. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop. Titles take a full stop when they are sentences: "You're here."

Spelling

British in the interface: behaviour, centre, colour, photograph. Code stays in its own conventions.

Equal weight

The way out is written as an offer: "Continue quietly", "Leave this practice", "Continue without a practice". Those take the place of "Skip", "Cancel" and "No thanks".

Nothing counted

Progress reads "1 of 3". Status lines report state: "Ocean is on until you stop it". Streaks, totals, days and "you have completed" stay out of the product.

Boundaries

Practices are described as optional general-wellness supports. The wellbeing line appears wherever a practice is offered in settings: "Stop if anything feels uncomfortable."

Privacy in plain words

"It does not collect personal information, track your browsing activity, or send anything to a server." Name any third party (Tally) and what it does.

Microcopy patterns

  • Status: subject, state, scope. "Ocean is on until you stop it." "Your personal photograph was removed. Open water is restored."
  • Errors: what happened, then what to try, no apology. "That photograph is larger than 15 MB. Choose a smaller copy."
  • Guidance: the instruction, then a softener. "Press your feet gently into the floor. Use only as much pressure as feels comfortable, then release."
  • Announcements: the same words as on screen, sent to the polite live region after a frame.

Rules

Do and don't

Do
  • Put text on a measured surface: glass, pill, or vignette.
  • Keep one blur on screen. Header pills stay opaque paper.
  • Pair every primary with an equal or underlined way out.
  • Use the alpha ladder. Reuse 0.075, 0.09, 0.10, 0.14, 0.18 before inventing.
  • Keep the journey heading at one size on every screen at a breakpoint.
  • Write British, plain, second person, no em dashes.
  • Measure contrast against the darkest and lightest ground a surface can meet.
  • Ship the mark inline, the font locally, and no third-party requests.
Don't
  • Add an accent colour, a gradient hero, or a second typeface.
  • Add icons to labels that fit as words, or icon-only buttons except the dialog close.
  • Show counts, streaks, history or "close the day" anywhere.
  • Start sound, voice, or video without a press.
  • Style the wordmark or the mark as a pill or a link target.
  • Stack two glass panels, or fade two panels over each other.
  • Use pixel spacing outside the five bands without adding the band to this page.

Maintenance

Maintenance notes

This section is for future edits. It is useful to keep with the system, and it sits behind the main story on the website.

Before shipping a visual change

Check the four built-in photographs and one bright personal photo; keyboard through the flow; test Still and reduced transparency; measure any new text surface; run the shorter phone heights.

Public publishing rule

Lead with the product thinking: sight, sound, body, restraint, accessibility and privacy. Keep implementation notes available, after the design decisions.

Show internal version and gap notes

Where the truth lives

Two builds share one stylesheet: the Chrome extension (Chrome Web Store, v0.25.18) and the web PWA (christingilbert.com/my-morning-door, v0.25.20). The web build adds an install pill and the phone rules at the end of the same file. When they disagree, the newer build is right and this page should follow it.

Version places

Extension: manifest.json and the version meta in newtab.html. Web: index.html meta plus its six ?v stamps, sw.js VERSION, web-shell.js VERSION. Bump on every deploy, even CSS-only.

Gaps to know about

The light glass theme is styled but unreachable. The base h1 rule is overridden everywhere. Spacing is written as literal pixels. Two historical file names no longer match the images they hold.
