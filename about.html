Why I love my work and what keeps me curious

I'm Christin Gilbert, a Senior Experience Designer based in Vancouver. I've always been fascinated by the boundaries of human cognition: how people compare, choose, pivot and rethink when information demands too much of our attention. My work spans experience design, content structure, cognitive accessibility and visual systems. I think about the structural aspects of the information and what can be removed to allow easier decision making.

Where it started

I began designing reservation interfaces in 2005. Those early screens taught me a lesson I still rely on today: every field and step influences how someone makes a decision. My background in clinical psychology and cognitive neuropsychology gave me the tools to articulate something I had observed for years: our attention and working memory have limits. The screens we interact with daily can either support those limits or make everything more difficult.

Recent work

Most recently, at Éminence Organics, I led the development of the systems behind digital creative production. My work connected campaign direction with the structures used to create, organize and deliver assets for the company's new B2B ecommerce site and other digital channels.

I developed and maintained a 90-page visual guide and DAM taxonomy, then expanded the guidance to include AI-assisted production. I remained involved in hands-on creative work, which kept the system grounded in how assets were actually made and used.

What I focus on now

My current work revolves around cognitive accessibility and calmer decision support. For me, cognitive accessibility means designing experiences that respect the limits of attention and working memory, so people can understand what is important to them without carrying unnecessary mental load.

Outside work

Photography, video, painting, mixed media and writing on Substack help keep me grounded. Sailing and exploring ancient pilgrimage routes help me find my bearings and make sense of the world around me.
