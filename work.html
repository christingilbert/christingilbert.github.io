Case Studies

These case studies examine cognitive load from two perspectives: the experiences people go through and the systems that support their work. Bezier Loupe turns the question into an instrument, so a design team can see what a screen asks of a person while the work is still easy to change. My Morning Door and Nookfare explore situations where attention is limited but a person still needs to make progress. The Éminence study explains how I created a creative production system over three years of campaign work.

01Bezier Loupe · Figma plugin · 2026

A Figma plugin that measures visual density and competing signals before development handoff

Bezier Loupe measures visual density and competing signals in a selected frame as Calm, Lively or Loud. Backed by 469 automated tests, its 12 checks show the measurement and source behind each finding and flag what needs designer judgement.

Product design · Cognitive accessibility · TypeScript

Coming soon · September 21

02My Morning Door · Chrome extension · 2026

A multi-sensory calm space for your new Chrome tab

My Morning Door turns Chrome's new tab into a quieter starting point using images I photographed. It combines ambient rain, ocean, forest and brown noise with optional voice guidance and short breathing, grounding or seated-release practices. No accounts, no analytics, and no browsing‑history access.

Experience design · Interaction design · Cognitive accessibility

03Nookfare · Live prototype · 2026

Choosing a stay with your sensory needs in mind

Nookfare starts with a traveller's sensory preferences, then uses them to shape how stays are presented and compared. Each stay is shown in relation to those preferences, with sensory details alongside practical information about arriving at the property.

Experience design · Sensory-first UX · Cognitive accessibility

How digital visual standards evolved over three years

From 2023–2026, I developed and maintained a 90-page visual style guide for Marketing Operations and Digital, while producing and managing a library of 6,000+ assets. Later updates added AI workflow guidance and guardrails.
